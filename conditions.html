1What YellowGo Ride is

YellowGo Ride is a platform that connects passengers with independent tricycle drivers. We help you find a nearby driver and track your trip — we are not the driver, and we do not own or operate the tricycles used on trips booked through the app.

2Your account

You need a valid phone number to create an account. You're responsible for keeping your password secure and for all activity under your account. Let us know right away if you think someone else has accessed it.

3Requesting a ride

When you request a ride, we share your pickup location (detected automatically from your device) and your typed destination with a nearby available driver. Once a driver accepts, you'll see their name, driver code, and live location until your trip ends.

4Fares are agreed directly with your driver

YellowGo Ride does not set or collect ride fares. You and your driver agree the price together, in person, before or during the trip, and you pay your driver directly — by cash or Mobile Money, whatever you both agree on. We add no commission or booking fee on top of your fare.

5Cancellations

You can cancel a ride request before a driver arrives. Repeated last-minute cancellations may affect your ability to request rides in the future, since it takes drivers away from other passengers.

6Conduct and safety

Treat your driver with respect. You can rate your trip and report any safety concerns, unsafe driving, or inappropriate behavior directly through the app after your ride. We review all reports and may suspend accounts — passenger or driver — that violate this.

7Location data

We use your device's location to set your pickup point and to show your driver's live position during an active trip. Location access can be turned off in your device settings, but you'll need to enter a pickup location manually if you do.

8Liability

Drivers on YellowGo Ride are independent and not our employees. While we verify driver identity and vehicle details before approval, we are not responsible for the conduct of drivers during a trip, or for the condition of their vehicle. Use common judgment, and report anything that doesn't feel right.

9Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as YellowGo Ride grows. If we make a significant change, we'll let you know in the app before it takes effect.

10Contact

Questions about these terms, or anything about your account, can be sent through the app's support contact or to our admin team directly.

Effective date: 16 July 2026 · Applies to all drivers using YellowGo Ride in Accra, Kumasi, and Tamale.

1What YellowGo Ride is

YellowGo Ride is a platform that connects independent tricycle drivers with nearby passengers. You are an independent driver, not an employee, contractor, or agent of YellowGo Ride. You choose when to go online, which requests to accept, and how you operate your vehicle.

2Verification requirements

Before you can go online and accept rides, you must submit:

  • A clear photo of your Ghana Card or other national ID
  • A selfie of yourself holding that ID
  • Your vehicle's plate number, model, and color

Verification is typically reviewed within 24 hours. We may reject or later revoke verification if documents are unclear, expired, or don't match your account details.

3The daily fee

To go online each day, you pay a flat daily fee via Mobile Money and submit your transaction reference for admin approval. This is a flat fee for platform access — YellowGo Ride does not take a percentage or commission from any fare you earn. Fees paid are non-refundable once submitted, except where an admin determines a payment was submitted in error.

4Fares are agreed directly with your passenger

You and your passenger agree the fare together — YellowGo Ride does not set, suggest, or collect ride fares. Payment happens directly between you and the passenger, by cash or Mobile Money, however you both agree.

5Accepting and completing trips

Once you accept a request, mark your status accurately as you approach, start, and complete the trip — passengers rely on this to know where you are. If you need to cancel a trip after accepting, use the in-app cancellation and select an honest reason; repeated cancellations may affect your standing on the platform.

6Conduct and safety

You're expected to drive safely, treat passengers with respect, and maintain your vehicle in safe working condition. Passengers can rate you and report safety concerns after each trip. Verified reports of unsafe driving, harassment, or dishonest conduct may result in suspension or permanent removal from the platform.

7Location data

While you're online, your live location is shared with passengers who've been matched with you, so they can track your approach. Location sharing stops automatically when you go offline.

8Ratings and standing

Your average rating is calculated automatically from passenger reviews and shown on your profile. Consistently low ratings, unresolved reports, or fee payments that repeatedly fail review may result in restricted access to the platform.

9Liability

As an independent driver, you are responsible for your vehicle, your driving, and compliance with any local licensing or transport regulations that apply to you. YellowGo Ride is a platform that connects you with passengers — it does not own your vehicle, employ you, or assume liability for incidents during a trip.

We may update these terms as YellowGo Ride grows, including fee amounts or verification requirements. We'll notify drivers in the app before significant changes take effect.
